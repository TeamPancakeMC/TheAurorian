When stars dotted this dark sky, I finally came to my senses from a wave of intense dizziness. Since embarking on my adventures, I had never felt as terrible as I did now. If not for the throbbing pain in my arm reminding me I was alive, I might have thought I had reached the heavenly realm.

Where is this place? Who am I? What should I do?

Such cliché questions.

After collecting myself, I began to recall my experiences. I am an adventurer. I heard rumors of a magical stone that could transport one to an entirely new realm. So, after enduring countless hardships, I found it. The moment I reached out and touched it, the entire world seemed to turn upside down, everything thrown into chaos. I only felt dizzy and disoriented, the air in my chest slowly being sucked out, only to be replaced by something foul-smelling. After that, I couldn't remember a thing. Damn, I really thought I was done for.

Now, I breathed in the wonderful, fresh air freely. This feeling of being alive was truly blissful, like completing an underground expedition and returning to the surface with untold precious treasures. Except, right now, I had no treasures in hand, and it seemed even my clothes weren't entirely intact. If someone saw me, the term "a glimpse of spring" might be fitting.

But that was no longer important, because I suddenly realized I was lying on a bed in someone else's house. Turning my head, I could see a magnificent sky outside the window, where stars made the whole world glitter. But that wasn't the most beautiful part; even more fantastical were the many gorgeous pink ribbons of light floating in the starry sky. Their glow wasn't strong, seeming to converge in the heavens, and by the time it filtered into the room, it had grown rather dim.

I tried to sit up—effortlessly. Only then did I notice the clothes I was wearing weren't mine. In terms of style, they were indeed somewhat more revealing compared to my usual attire, no wonder I felt uncomfortable earlier. But aside from that, the bed was of excellent quality, very soft. I swear it was the most comfortable bed I had ever slept in. A faint firelight flickered, illuminating the pattern on the quilt—exactly like the ribbons of light in the sky.

If I wasn't mistaken, I was probably in the attic of someone's house. After checking my physical condition briefly, I concluded I was perfectly fine. There was no point sitting on the bed any longer. Moreover, out of courtesy, I should go downstairs soon to see my hosts. Even if I had nothing proper to offer them, at least I shouldn't cause them any more trouble.

Stretching slightly, I got up and observed the room's interior. A simple but warm and soft small bed—already mentioned. Then, a small window, outside which seemed to be a street. The window's craftsmanship was no different from those back home, perhaps even better. Looking further into the room, there was a small table, probably only big enough for a few small bowls. But on it, there was only a short candle, which seemed to have been burning for a while, wax solidified in the candlestick forming a white puddle.

The light from outside the window fell into the room, sharing the space equally with the small candlelight. One side was a faint pink, like a soft dreamscape; the other side had the small candlelight, feeling warm and cozy. This harmonious scene was indeed rare for someone who spent most of their time roaming amongst ruins or in desolate wilderness.

The strange sky, the warm bed, the small room, plus this casual, cute sleepwear—for a moment, I almost thought I wasn't a weapon-wielding maniac running around everywhere, but a rather pretty young girl.

Forget it, that word doesn't suit an adventurer.

Patting my head, still somewhat dazed from sleep, I turned my gaze back to the wondrous scene outside the window.

How to describe such a view? If I had to say, it was like being in some secret realm, a world utterly unique, found nowhere else. Bustling streets, noisy children, travelers coming and going, warriors with swords and shields—all existed under a pink-purple canopy, each in their element. The drifting ribbons of light swam in the sky, while the dazzling stars could only serve as embellishments to highlight their splendor. Birds occasionally flew past, cutting through the quiet night sky.

It's a pity I'm not skilled at writing, otherwise, even a simple description of this scene could lift a reader's spirits. If a poet could witness this sight, they would surely compose peerless verses for people to recite. Faced with such beauty, I nearly forgot my own awful experience.

After admiring the spectacle outside, I turned my attention back to the room's decor. Things I had overlooked before now entered my view.

In the corner of the room sat a chest, unlocked, needing just a slight push to open. Unfortunately, I couldn't see what was inside very well, so I turned to fetch the candlestick from the table. Holding it close, I saw my equipment neatly arranged inside: robes, hood, belt, clothes, the gem said to bring luck, and some gold pieces.

Most of the things I brought when I left were stored inside. The kind hosts hadn't taken my belongings but had instead sorted them and placed them here. Thinking this, I picked up the gem glittering in the candlelight and gently blew off any possible dust. Perhaps sensing I was awake, someone opened the hatch from below, producing a "creak" sound.
